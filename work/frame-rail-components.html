Frame Rail Components

Long structural frame rails cut weld-ready off the tube laser — 40 ft outbound capacity used in full, tight tolerances held across multi-piece weldments.

01 · The Challenge

What made this hard.

The customer's frame rails ran longer than most regional shops could handle — 30+ feet, often combined with mid-span notches, end miters, and through-holes for cross-member attachment. Their previous workflow was: cut long, transport short, hand-locate features. Slow and error-prone.

Weldment downstream meant tolerances on hole-to-hole and miter-to-miter dimensions had to hold across the full length to avoid downstream fit-up issues at assembly.

02 · What We Did

Process & approach.

Tube Laser Mitering BLM 40 ft

Routed the entire family to our BLM tube laser cell, which handles up to 40 ft outbound and cuts structural tube, beams, C-channels, and angles in a single setup. End miters, mid-span features, and through-holes get cut in one pass — weld-ready off the machine with no secondary fixturing.

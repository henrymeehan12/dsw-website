01 · The Challenge

What made this hard.

Ag implement frames demand consistency across seasonal volume swings — the OEM ramps up hard in [FILL: spring / fall planting season], then runs lower volume the rest of the year. Manual press brake operations couldn't hold dimensional consistency across operator shifts at peak volume.

The parts also involved plasma-cut bevel preps for downstream welding, adding setup complexity any time the part mix shifted.

02 · What We Did

Process & approach.

Plasma Bevel Robotic Bend A36 Plate

Plasma cutting for plate + bevel prep, then the robotic bend cell for the forming step — both processes give a repeatability that operator-driven press brakes can't match at this volume. Fixtures designed in-house. Run rate scales with cell capacity, not headcount.

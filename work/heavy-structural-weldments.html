Heavy Structural Weldments

Complex multi-piece weldments built to print, dimensionally verified with FARO arm CMM — assemblies up to [FILL: weight] in heavy plate.

01 · The Challenge

What made this hard.

The customer's weldments combine heavy plate cuts, formed components, and tube subassemblies into a single structure — each piece dimensionally fine on its own, but historically prone to stack-up errors at assembly. Previous supplier required field-fitting at the customer's plant.

PPAP-level documentation was a contract requirement: dimensional inspection of every weldment in early runs and statistical sampling in production.

02 · What We Did

Process & approach.

Robotic Weld Manual MIG Heavy Plate FARO CMM

Siegmund welding tables let us reference every weldment to a common datum across the build, eliminating accumulated stack-up. FARO arm CMM verifies dimensional pass against the print before the part leaves the bay. Robotic welding for the repeating stitch passes; manual welds on the critical seams.

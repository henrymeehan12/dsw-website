Enclosure & Rack Components

Precision laser-cut panels and formed enclosures for mission-critical installs — stainless and aluminum, tight cosmetic and dimensional tolerances.

01 · The Challenge

What made this hard.

Enclosure work for data center and power infrastructure has the unforgiving combination of tight cosmetic tolerances and visible material. Scratches, oil marks, mis-located cutouts — all of it shows. Customer specs called out stainless and aluminum with no rework allowed on the visible face.

Lead time pressure was real: enclosure orders typically arrive coupled to a project schedule, and a late shipment cascades downstream.

02 · What We Did

Process & approach.

Fiber Laser Stainless 304 Aluminum 6061 Press Brake

Routed to our fiber laser with tower-fed automation — protective film stays on through cutting, parts come off the bed already staged for forming with no operator handling on the cosmetic face. Press brake tooling dialed for stainless and aluminum with poly-jaw and felt-faced backstops.

Structural Bracket Assembly

6,200+ parts per month at consistent first-piece quality — PPAP-submitted, running production for [FILL: years] consecutive years.

01 · The Challenge

What made this hard.

The customer needed a high-volume structural bracket family produced to PPAP standards — multiple SKUs sharing a base material spec but with varying hole patterns and bend geometries. Their previous supplier missed first-piece on three consecutive PPAPs and had inconsistent on-time delivery.

The bracket geometry put it right at the boundary of laser plus single-axis press brake versus a robotic bend approach. The right call wasn't obvious without running test parts.

02 · What We Did

Process & approach.

Fiber Laser Press Brake Deburr PPAP

We ran first-piece samples on both processes, validated dimensional results with the FARO arm CMM, and converged on a fiber laser cut + manual press brake setup with custom tooling. Fixture design reduced setup time per SKU swap by [FILL: %]. PPAP package submitted and approved on first attempt.

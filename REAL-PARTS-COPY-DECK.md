## Claims to verify before your boss sees it

These are the lines someone will push on. Worth being right the first time.

1. **Paint / galvanizing is not in-house.** Your capabilities page correctly calls it "Partner Vendor Services." So card 6 says *"finished before it ships"* and the pill is *"Finishing Managed"* — never "in-house coating." If a customer audits and finds the coater is a third party after the site implied otherwise, that's the kind of thing that ends a program.
2. **CNC machining is new.** Capabilities page says "Recently Installed" and "full production specs will publish as the cells come fully online." Card 6 says "machined" as a process, not "years of machining experience." Keep it there until the cells are proven.
3. **The new welding cell isn't bought yet.** Card 1's "robot cells" must describe the OTC Daihen cells you're running **today**, not the Lincoln/Capital cell still under evaluation. The plural wording covers you as long as the existing cells run weldments of this type.
4. **Customer confidentiality — the binding constraint.** Kingpin nests are out. The test I applied to every card: could a competitor or customer read this and identify one account? A specific part name plus a specific annual volume usually can, even with no customer named. That's why no card carries a volume figure. If you *want* numbers, use shop-wide ones (total parts/month across all programs, on-time %) — those are defensible and identify nobody.
5. **"Both shifts."** Confirm the automation-fed lasers and robotic brake actually run both shifts today. Capabilities page says "near-24/7" and "across both shifts," so this is probably fine — just confirm it's still true.

Either finish them, or keep them out of the deploy until the numbers are real.
2. **Two industries on the homepage have no Work page section.** "Industries Served" lists seven; `work.html` has five. **Construction & Infrastructure** and **Material Handling** are missing. Card 2 needs the Construction anchor, so this gets fixed either way.
3. **"See All Work →" goes to an industry overview, not work.** The Work page is five industry blurbs with Contact buttons. A buyer clicking "See All Work" expects to see parts. Lowest-effort fix: link the finished case pages from it once the numbers are in.
